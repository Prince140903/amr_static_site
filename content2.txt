<strong>Coarse Filtration:</strong> This stage is designed to remove large particles such as dirt, sand, rust and other particulates. This is usually done with a filter screen or a sand filter. </p><p>2. <strong>Fine Filtration:</strong> This stage is designed to remove smaller particles such as bacteria, algae, and viruses. This is usually done with a pleated filter. </p><p>3. <strong>Deionization:</strong> This stage is designed to remove dissolved ions such as calcium, magnesium, sodium and other dissolved minerals. This is usually done with an ion exchange or a reverse osmosis filter. </p><p>4. <strong>Disinfection: </strong>This stage is designed to kill any remaining bacteria and viruses.
